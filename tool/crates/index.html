Crate Reference

The workspace has eleven crates. Each library exposes its surface from src/lib.rs through pub use re-exports, pub fn items and public types; the pages below list that surface with its responsibilities.

vmpx-pe ---+--> vmpx-detect
           +--> vmpx-unpack
           +--> vmpx-rebuild
           +--> vmpx-disasm ---> vmpx-vm ---+
                                            +--> vmpx-vmlift
vmpx-recompile -----------------------------+

vmpx-cli depends on all of the above and produces the vmpx binary;
vmpx-ui drives that binary as a child process.

Role

Guarded PE rewriting and reintegration

all of the above, clap, serde, serde_json, sha2, anyhow

eframe, rfd, serde_json, anyhow

thiserror

Feature-gated FFI to the VTIL bridge

Workspace-wide settings

[workspace.package]
version = "0.1.0"
edition = "2024"
license = "MIT"
rust-version = "1.88"

[workspace.lints.rust]
unsafe_code = "forbid"

[workspace.lints.clippy]
all = "warn"
pedantic = "warn"

unsafe_code = "forbid" applies to every crate except vmpx-vtil-sys, which opts out (unsafe_code = "allow") because it is the only crate that performs FFI.
